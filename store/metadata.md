## Description

**ddairy — a softer place for your days.**

Speak your diary. Press the mic, talk about your day, and watch your words appear,
punctuated and tidy. No account, no cloud — your pages live on your device.

A VOICE JOURNAL THAT FEELS LIKE A DIARY
• Dictate entries with your device's built-in speech recognition (or the browser on web)
• Spoken punctuation: say "comma", "new paragraph", "full stop"
• Fillers like "um" and "huh" are caught and removed; waves show you that sound is loading
• Type whenever typing feels better

ONE MAIN DIARY + ALL YOUR BOOKS
• A main diary for your days, ready at first open
• Create books for trips, ideas, work, healing — each with its own icon and color
• Switch books instantly from anywhere

GENTLE PROMPTS & TAGS
• Guided prompt chips: "Three things I'm grateful for", "A small win from today"
• Tags suggested from your own words: gratitude, reflection, memories, mindfulness...
• Mood check-ins with a soft palette

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is stored on your device
• No account, no analytics, no uploads
• Export any book to Markdown whenever you want

MADE FOR EVERY SCREEN
• Comfortable on phones and expansive on tablets, in light and dark modes

Try speaking tomorrow's first page — starting the habit is the only hard part.
